PERSONALITY + AI ATTITUDES

A short interactive assessment for a pilot research exercise.

This study explores Big Five personality traits and two exploratory tendencies related to AI: willingness to adopt useful AI tools and critical checking of AI outputs.

What we collect

Your responses to 30 rating-scale items, automatically calculated scale scores, completion timestamps, and optional usability feedback. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, employer, or other unnecessary identifying information.

How it is used

Responses are stored for aggregate analysis in an admin dashboard. This is a pilot for educational/research exploration, not a validated diagnostic test and not a clinical or medical assessment.

Consent

Participation is voluntary. You may stop before submitting. By continuing, you confirm that you understand the purpose and agree to anonymous storage and aggregate analysis of your responses.
